DeltaLead connects to your existing sales stack without requiring you to migrate data or replace any of your current tools. Whether you're running Salesforce, HubSpot, a DMS like Tecnom, or simply Google Sheets, you plug DeltaLead in on top — so qualified leads, AI summaries, scores, and conversation history flow into wherever your team already works.

## Integration categories

DeltaLead covers every layer of your dealership's tech stack — from the CRMs your sales team lives in to the ad platforms generating your leads.

| Category | Tools |
|---|---|
| **CRM** | Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, Odoo, Bitrix |
| **DMS (automotive)** | Tecnom, PilotSolution |
| **Communication** | WhatsApp, Email (Gmail), Slack, Microsoft Teams |
| **Lead sources** | Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads, MercadoLibre, Typeform, web forms |
| **Workflow** | Zapier, Make, Notion, Trello, Jira, ClickUp |
| **Custom** | Webhooks, REST API |

<Note>
  Standard integrations — including all CRMs, DMS systems, lead sources, and communication channels listed above — are included in all plans (Growth, Advanced, and Enterprise) at no extra cost. Custom integrations built to connect proprietary or unlisted systems are available on the Enterprise plan and are scoped during onboarding.
</Note>
