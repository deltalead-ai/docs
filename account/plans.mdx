## Monthly Active Client (MAC) Billing

DeltaLead bills on **Monthly Active Clients (MACs)**, not on message volume or seat count. Understanding how a MAC is defined helps you size your plan accurately.

**A MAC is any lead with at least one activity during the calendar month** — an inbound message, an outbound follow-up, a WhatsApp call, or any action by a seller inside the platform.

Key rules:

- **One lead = one MAC per month, regardless of volume.** A lead that received an initial message, seven follow-ups, two calls, and twenty back-and-forth messages counts as exactly one MAC — the same as a lead you exchanged two messages with.
- **Leads only count in months they are active.** A lead that entered your pipeline in August and receives a follow-up in October counts once in August and once in October. September costs nothing if there was no activity.
- **Outreach with no response still counts.** Initiating a conversation consumes Meta costs and platform resources whether or not the lead replies.

### Overage Pricing

You are never blocked or throttled when you exceed your plan's included MAC volume. You simply pay for the overage:

| Plan | Overage rate |
|---|---|
| Growth | US$12 per 10 additional MACs |
| Advanced | US$15 per 10 additional MACs |
| Enterprise | Defined in contract |

This means you can size your plan for your normal monthly volume and only pay the spike when it happens — for example, during a promotional sales event. If overage becomes consistent over several months, it makes sense to move up a tier.

<Note>
  Your plan's included MAC volume is scoped to **new leads plus the portion of older leads still in active follow-up**. If you generate 2,000 new leads a month and 25% of previous leads remain active, your real working volume is closer to 2,500 MACs. [Request a simulation](https://www.deltalead.ai/es-ar/contacto) before you sign and DeltaLead will model it against your actual numbers.
</Note>

## Annual vs. Monthly Billing

All plans are available on monthly billing with no minimum commitment. The annual plan exists because it saves you 20% — not because it is required.

The typical path is: start your 14-day free trial, continue month-to-month while you validate results with your own leads, then switch to annual whenever you are ready to lock in the discount. **You can switch from monthly to annual at any time.**
