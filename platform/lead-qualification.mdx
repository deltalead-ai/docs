DeltaLead's AI agents qualify every incoming lead the moment they make contact — regardless of channel, time of day, or whether your sales team is available. The AI greets the lead naturally, asks the right questions, scores them by purchase intent, budget, and urgency, and hands your sellers a ready-to-close opportunity complete with a full summary. Your team stops triaging inboxes and starts closing deals.

## Qualification flow

Every lead goes through the same structured journey, handled entirely by the AI until your seller is needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lead enters from any channel">
    A new contact arrives via WhatsApp, Meta Ads, MercadoLibre, a web form, Typeform, email, or a phone call. DeltaLead captures it instantly and creates or updates a unified lead profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI greets and opens the conversation">
    The agent responds in under 9 seconds — any hour, any day including holidays. It introduces itself naturally, matches your dealership's tone and regional language variant, and opens the conversation without making the lead feel like they're talking to a bot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI asks qualifying questions naturally">
    The agent guides the conversation to surface the information your sellers need: which vehicle model the lead is interested in, their budget range, whether they have a trade-in, their preferred financing method, and their buying timeline. Questions feel conversational, not interrogative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI scores by intent, budget, and urgency">
    Based on the conversation, DeltaLead assigns a lead score across three dimensions — purchase intent (how ready to buy), budget fit (does their range match your inventory), and urgency (how soon they want to move). High-scoring leads are surfaced immediately for your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI summary generated for the seller">
    Once the qualifying conversation concludes, the AI generates a structured summary: vehicle of interest, budget, financing preferences, trade-in details, timeline, and any specific commitments made during the conversation. This summary appears at the top of the lead's profile in your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller receives hot leads ready to close">
    Your seller opens a lead card and sees everything they need to have a meaningful first human conversation — no repeated questions, no cold starts. They pick up exactly where the AI left off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seller context panel

When a seller takes over a conversation, they never start blind. The context panel at the top of every lead profile shows everything the AI gathered.

<Accordion title="What the seller sees when they take over">
  | Field | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **AI summary** | A plain-language paragraph summarizing the full conversation and the lead's intent level |
  | **Vehicle of interest** | Model, trim, color preferences, and any specific questions asked |
  | **Financing preferences** | Cash, loan, or installments — and the number of installments requested if applicable |
  | **Trade-in** | Whether the lead has a vehicle to trade in, and what model they mentioned |
  | **Buying timeline** | "This week," "this month," or longer — extracted from the conversation |
  | **What was promised** | Any commitments the AI made: follow-up dates, pricing information shared, test drives confirmed |
  | **Next suggested action** | The AI's recommendation for what the seller should do next based on the lead's profile |
</Accordion>

The seller can also review the complete conversation transcript at any time before or during their interaction. For phone leads, call recordings and automatic transcripts are included.

## Automatic follow-up

Leads that don't convert on first contact rarely disappear — they just need the right nudge at the right time. DeltaLead's follow-up sequences keep every lead active without any manual effort from your team.

Configure your cadences to match your sales strategy:

- **Number of follow-ups** — set how many times the AI reaches out before marking a lead as inactive
- **Days between messages** — space follow-ups according to buying cycle length; short for high-urgency leads, longer for research-phase contacts
- **Channel selection** — follow up via WhatsApp, email, or a combination based on how the lead originally came in

Follow-up sequences stop automatically when any of the following occurs:

- The lead responds to a message
- The lead books a test drive or showroom visit
- A seller manually takes ownership of the conversation
- The lead is marked as lost

<Warning>
  **Protect your WhatsApp number health.** Sending follow-up messages to leads who never respond can degrade your Meta number quality score — and a low-quality number can eventually be restricted or blocked by Meta, cutting off your primary sales channel. DeltaLead configures your follow-up cadences during onboarding to keep engagement rates healthy and your number safe. High-intent leads receive more follow-ups; cold or unresponsive contacts receive fewer. Do not configure aggressive bulk cadences on your own without consulting your DeltaLead onboarding specialist.
</Warning>
