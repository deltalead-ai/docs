## Billing by Active-Lead Bands

Growth and Advanced are priced by **bands of active leads per month** — not by message volume or seat count. Each plan has a price per band, and you size the band to your dealership's usual monthly volume.

**An active lead is any lead with at least one activity during the calendar month** — an inbound message, an outbound follow-up, a WhatsApp call, or any action by a seller inside the platform.

Key rules:

- **A lead counts once per month, regardless of volume.** A lead that received an initial message, seven follow-ups, two calls, and twenty back-and-forth messages counts as one active lead — the same as a lead you exchanged two messages with.
- **Leads only count in months they are active.** A lead that entered your pipeline in August and receives a follow-up in October counts in August and in October. September adds nothing if there was no activity.
- **Starter has no lead limit.** The Starter plan includes unlimited active leads.
- **Growth and Advanced bands go up to 10,000 active leads per month.** Above that volume, Enterprise applies.

### Overage

You are never blocked or throttled when you exceed your band. Additional active leads are billed as overage per block of 100 leads, at a rate that depends on your plan. Current rates are on the [pricing page](https://www.deltalead.ai/es-ar/precios/delta-crm/).

This means you can size your band for your normal monthly volume and only pay the spike when it happens — for example, during a promotional sales event. If overage becomes consistent over several months, it makes sense to move up a band.

<Note>
  Your active-lead volume is **new leads plus the portion of older leads still in active follow-up**. If you generate 2,000 new leads a month and 25% of previous leads remain active, your real volume is closer to 2,500 active leads. [Request a simulation](https://www.deltalead.ai/es-ar/contacto) before you sign and DeltaLead will model it against your actual numbers.
</Note>

## WhatsApp Costs: Billed by Meta

DeltaLead connects to WhatsApp through **Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API**. Messages, templates (marketing, utility, and authentication), and WhatsApp calls are billed **by Meta directly to your Meta Business account**, at Meta's rates and with no DeltaLead markup.

- **DeltaLead charges nothing per message, per template, or per call minute.** Your plan price neither includes nor resells Meta costs.
- **You need your own Meta Business account with a payment method set up in Meta.** That is the account Meta bills for WhatsApp usage.
- **Customer-initiated WhatsApp calls are free** under Meta's rules. Business-initiated calls may carry a Meta charge.
- **Your WhatsApp number is always your own**, registered on your own WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). DeltaLead does not rent numbers.

See Meta's current rates by template category and country in [Meta's WhatsApp Business pricing documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing).

## Annual vs. Monthly Billing

All plans are billed in US dollars (USD), on monthly billing with no minimum commitment. The annual plan exists because it saves you 20% — not because it is required.

The typical path is: start your 14-day free trial, continue month-to-month while you validate results with your own leads, then switch to annual whenever you are ready to lock in the discount. **You can switch from monthly to annual at any time.**
