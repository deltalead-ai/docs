Getting DeltaLead running in your dealership takes between one week and one month, depending on the complexity of your operation. A single-location dealership connecting WhatsApp and one CRM can be live in a week. A multi-agency setup with custom integrations, multiple lead sources, and a specialized commercial playbook typically takes three to four weeks. Either way, nothing in your current operation is interrupted — agents go live channel by channel and hour block by hour block, so you stay in control throughout.

## What You Need Before Starting

Prepare the following before your first onboarding session. These are the only three things DeltaLead needs from you — the rest is handled by the implementation team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access to your WhatsApp Business number">
    You will need to connect your existing WhatsApp Business number to the platform. Once connected, the number is managed through DeltaLead — you will stop using it directly in the WhatsApp Business app. If you prefer not to migrate your current number, you can set up a new SIM or eSIM: you only need to place it in a device for about five minutes to complete Meta's verification, and then you can remove it. WhatsApp calls work over this same number with no additional configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your vehicle catalog or stock spreadsheet">
    Bring your inventory in any format — a spreadsheet, a DMS export, a CSV, or even a manually maintained list. DeltaLead uses this to train the AI on your actual stock so it never offers a unit that has already been sold or reserved. You can update the catalog after go-live; this initial version is just the starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A team member available for commercial questions during the first week">
    The AI configuration team will need to understand how your dealership sells: what objections are common, which discounts you authorize, when to hand off to a human advisor, and what tone fits your brand. Having a sales manager or senior advisor available to answer these questions during the first week ensures the AI reflects your actual commercial playbook — not a generic one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Gradual Activation Strategy

You do not need to flip a switch and hand everything over to the AI on day one. DeltaLead recommends — and is designed for — a phased activation that lets you build confidence before expanding coverage.

**Recommended sequence:**

1. **Start with overnight and weekend hours.** Activate the AI agent only during the hours when your team is not available — late nights, early mornings, and weekends. These are hours where you currently have zero coverage, so there is nothing to disrupt and everything to gain.
2. **Measure results for two to four weeks.** Review response rates, lead qualification scores, appointments booked, and how the AI handles real conversations with your actual leads. Adjust the playbook, tone, and follow-up cadence based on what you see.
3. **Expand to full hours.** Once you are satisfied with how the AI performs, extend coverage to business hours. Your team continues handling escalations and human handoffs — the AI handles first contact and follow-up.

This approach means your current operations run without disruption at every stage. Sellers work alongside the AI from the start; they are not replaced or bypassed.

<Tip>
  Start with a single channel — WhatsApp is the highest-volume channel for most dealerships — and add Meta Ads, MercadoLibre, and other sources once you have seen results on the first one. Trying to configure every channel simultaneously slows down the initial setup and makes it harder to identify what is working.
</Tip>
