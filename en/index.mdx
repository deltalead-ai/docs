## How onboarding works

Get from sign-up to your first qualified lead in four steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your channels">
    Link your WhatsApp Business number, Meta Ads account, MercadoLibre listings, web forms, and email inboxes to DeltaLead. No code required — each channel connects through a guided wizard in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI qualifies your leads">
    As soon as a new inquiry arrives, your AI agent responds in under 9 seconds — 24/7, including weekends and holidays. It asks the right questions to score each lead by purchase intent, budget, and urgency, then hands off to your sales team when the moment is right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review scored leads">
    Open your unified inbox and see every lead ranked by score. Each profile shows the full conversation history across all channels — WhatsApp, calls, email, and more — so your team picks up the conversation with full context, never asking a buyer to repeat themselves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync to your CRM">
    Push qualified leads and all their activity automatically to Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, Odoo, Bitrix, PilotSolution, Tecnom, or Google Sheets. Your CRM stays up to date without manual data entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>
